# Stuck order follow-up

**Done when:** Customer contacted and a note added to the order, or the order cancelled after 21 days.

## Steps

1. Pull the order, customer contact and pro forma invoice.
2. Check whether a payment arrived but was not matched.
3. Draft the follow-up email from the template; sales operations approve and send.
4. Add a note to the order with the contact date.
5. After 21 days propose cancellation and stock release.
